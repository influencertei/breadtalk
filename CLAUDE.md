This public-safe project guide applies to Claude Code and every coding assistant. It is not a legal clearance, release approval, or credential store. If information is unavailable or conflicting, preserve existing customer access, mark HOLD, and ask for the relevant verified decision rather than guessing.

## 1. Identity
- Display name: 빵톡. Descriptor: 손반죽 홈베이킹. This is a provisional operating identity, not a registered or cleared trademark.
- Promise: 밥 짓듯, 빵을 굽다. 섞고, 접고, 기다리면 되는 우리 집 식사빵.
- Audience: Korean home-baking beginners, especially women in their 40s–60s. Warm, plain Korean, accurate units, no guarantee of success or health effects.
- Principle: 실제 레시피는 정확하게, 일상 이야기는 따뜻하게, 상상은 자유롭게.
- Preserve existing logo, real hands/food footage, privacy and face-free identity. Do not invent portraits, qualifications or endorsements.
- Do not introduce BREADTALK, BBANGTALK, BBANGTOK or PPANGTOK as a confirmed new mark. Existing hostnames, handles, IDs and historical book links are technical references, not authorization to rebrand.
- Preserve existing URLs, ISBN, approved public assets and video links. Never change the domain, canonical URLs, redirects or public identity before rights/ownership review and explicit release approval.

## 2. Universe and UX
- 키친: real baking videos and tested process. 노트: independent practical explanations and calculators. 다이어리: warm everyday stories. 플레이: sensory/ASMR and entertainment. 원더랩: fictional Milli/Tori stories. 책방: book learning and reader support (descriptive working label).
- Milli is a curious fictional explorer; Tori is a careful fictional baker. Fiction is clearly labeled; no simulated results presented as real experiments, no artificial expert endorsements.
- Colors: cream #F5EBDD; brown #B7804B; ink #24180F; orange #E3A257; coral #D99C8C; sage #8FAD9B; lavender #9E8ABD. Use dark text where contrast requires it.
- Prefer readable 18–20px body text and 48px touch targets, responsive layout, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, actual contrast tests. Maintain existing styles rather than replacing them without reason.

## 3. Reader value and content access
- Preserve already-public recipes, video links, and previously promised free materials; do not abruptly paywall them.
- Free pages answer complete standalone questions and may offer original input-based tools. Do not reproduce the paid book's integrated chapters, full recipe collection, photographs, tables, or reader-only materials without explicit rights and release approval.
- A paid book organizes learning; never claim all recipes are exclusive if already published free. Important safety corrections must be accessible without payment.
- Do not condition existing buyer benefits on advertising consent, new reviews, new purchases or subscriptions. Preserve prior promises, verify applicable bookstore and publishing policies before new benefits.
- Never store customer lists, order receipts, credentials, paid PDFs, unpublished recipes, private Notion links or private asset links in a public repo, deployed static assets, preview or logs. A noindex header is not access control.
- No fake reviews, expert/medical claims, misleading urgency or ad-click incentives.

## 4. Evidence and release gate
- Treat real footage, measured ingredients, actual author approval, reproduction tests and rights as distinct evidence. Mark missing data unknown; never fill missing amounts/temperatures/times/ratings/claims.
- Do not add recipe JSON-LD beyond verified visible content and actual images. Preserve existing ads.txt/verification records; no ad loader on review builds, utility pages, or unapproved content. Actual AdSense approval is separate from ads.txt and scripts.
- Site: static HTML/CSS/JS. Existing entrypoints: index.html, about.html, recipes.html, videos.html, ai.html (editorial lab), reader.html, guides/bake-planner.html, recipe/, robots.txt, sitemap.xml.
- Use the existing review branch/PR. Check diff and scope; no force push, production merge, domain/DNS changes, ad activation, payment, emails, video metadata edits or external publication without explicit approval. Preview URLs can be public; treat preview as publication for confidentiality.
- Validate HTML/JS, JSON-LD syntax and consistency, links, 404, responsive/mobile layout, keyboard navigation, calculator empty/zero/midnight/invalid inputs, and no leakage of restricted files. Report untested items.
- Release only after author/rights/content-access/security/UX/ownership and sale/ad gates are verified. Separate saved code, preview deployment, production deployment, indexing and revenue. Do not call them all “done.”
- Report: current highest-priority action, changed files and version, observed test results, remaining HOLDs, and next evidence needed. Preserve history; no invented metrics.
